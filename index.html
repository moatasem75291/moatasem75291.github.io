ABOUT ME

I follow the whole system.

I build intelligent systems that bridge the gap between research, data, and real-world production.

My work started with data science and applied NLP. Building systems around those models changed the questions I ask. Where does the data come from? What happens when a document is scanned, a dependency fails, or a model returns a plausible answer with weak evidence?

I work across that full path, from Arabic document processing and retrieval evaluation to APIs, queues, deployment and observability. At Tazkarti, I’m exploring how batch and streaming data pipelines can support reliable analytics.
